- Security: a session the daemon starts for itself is now bounded by the session it descends from (CW-20261016-0218, part A). A resumed session may not hold more scopes than the session it continues, even if that session's credential has since ended, and a team member (or its replacement) may not hold more than its parent session; the team host starts members through a new `LaunchInternal`, whose origin marker only daemon code can set. One helper decides this on both the hub-local and the worker credential paths. A launch with no verified principal and no origin is classified `anonymous_external`, and a daemon launch with no origin session `internal_unattributed`; both still receive the profile's scopes as before, and the audit rows and counters for them follow in part B.
