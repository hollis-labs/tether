- A busy or offline recipient is no longer hammered by wake retries (CW-20261030-0176): the delay doubles from its base (5 s busy, 30 s offline) up to 300 s with +/-20% jitter, a delivery gets 12 attempts before the delay jumps to the message-age bound (the mail stays in the inbox, the recipient pulls it), and one sweep pass makes at most one wake attempt per busy session instead of one per mail. Already read or consumed mail still stops the retries as before. Not in this change: an idle-triggered wake when the session leaves busy.
