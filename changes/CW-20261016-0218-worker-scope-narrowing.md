- Security: a worker-hosted session's credential and launch spec are now limited to the scopes the launching principal itself holds, as a hub-local session's always were (CW-20261016-0218). Before, a scoped launcher (for example a session token without `*`) that launched onto a worker got the agent profile's scopes, which could be broader than its own. A launcher holding `*` keeps the profile's scopes; an anonymous launch (identity observe or off only) keeps the profile's scopes as before. Both credential paths share one helper. Sessions already running keep the scopes they were given.
