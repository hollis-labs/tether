- Security: team session launches now record the authority they were first launched under, so recovery and reconcile launches, which have no verified caller, are bounded by it instead of being unattributed (CW-20261016-0233, migration 0089 adds `team_launch_grants`). Without this a global `identity.mode: enforce` would refuse every team recovery launch as `internal_unattributed`. The first launch under a verified caller records that caller's scopes (first write wins); later launches of the same intent reuse them, together with the parent session's grant when there is one. A root team session formed before this has no grant and, under global enforce, is refused with a logged reason. The I6 runbook gains a read-only pre-flip query that counts such sessions. A recorded grant is void once the principal that held it is revoked or expired: its teams then stop recovering, with a refusal and a log line, instead of outliving the revocation.
