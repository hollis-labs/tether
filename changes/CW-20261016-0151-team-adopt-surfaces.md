- Teams: `tether team adopt`, `POST /teams/adopt` and `tether_team_adopt` (CW-20261016-0151, part 3 of 3). With `adoption: true` in `teams-policy.yaml` the daemon builds a registry-backed adopter that verifies the actor, its live binding generation, its running session, the launcher named by the session's own credential and its allowlisted launch pin from the daemon's own records; adopted members are released, never stopped, when a run ends or they are removed. New `identity.Store.SessionOwner`. See docs/teams-activation.md, "Adopting running agents".
