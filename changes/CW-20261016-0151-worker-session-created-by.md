- Sessions: a session launched on a worker now records who launched it on its credential (`created_by`), as a hub-local session always did (CW-20261016-0151). Worker-hosted sessions launched since the worker session credential landed (about Oct 12) had an empty `created_by`, so nothing could show they belonged to their launcher and the operator could not adopt them into a team. Sessions that already exist keep their empty value; their credentials cannot be re-minted once running.
