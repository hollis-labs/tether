- Teams: `teamsvc.Service.Adopt` and the `adoption: true` teams-policy opt-in (CW-20261016-0151, part 2 of 3; the real adopter, CLI/MCP verb and docs follow). The run owner can adopt an already-running agent and its session into a pool seat the run's definition already declares for that actor, within the slot's size. The coordinator seat is held only as the owner's delegate: the team's grants from that slot may be `may_message` and `may_assign` only (never `may_delegate`, `may_admin` or `may_spawn`), the delegate cannot dissolve, remove or adopt, and removing it does not end the run. Dissolving or removing an adopted member releases it and never stops its session or releases its binding. With adoption on, the Stage A shape also admits empty pool seats (workers 0..2, reviewer 0..1, coordinator owner plus one delegate); the reviewer runtime rule still applies through the slot pins.
