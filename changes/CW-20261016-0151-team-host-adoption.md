- Team host: `Host.Adopt` records an already-running, enrolled actor and its live session as a team member without provisioning it (CW-20261016-0151, part 1 of 3: host store and lifecycle; the service verb, policy and CLI follow). Adoption is verified from the session's own records (resolved binding owned by the forming principal, allowlisted launch, exact definition pin), names the binding generation, and is a compare-and-set: one active team per actor and per session. Releasing an adopted member (dissolve, remove, timeout) revokes its team enrollment and sends one notice but never stops the session or touches its registry binding; a missing adopter fails closed. A rebound session is suspended until explicitly re-adopted. Migration 0084 adds `team_host_adoptions`.
