# Tether Trademark and Brand Guidelines

"Tether", "Hollis Labs", and related Tether branding are trademarks of
Chrispian Burks / Hollis Labs.

This project is open source under the MIT License. You are free to use,
modify, and distribute the code for any purpose, including commercial use.

However, the Tether name, logo, and branding are not covered by the MIT
License.

## Allowed Uses (No Permission Required)

The following uses are allowed without additional permission:

- "Built with Tether"
- "Powered by Tether"
- Attribution in documentation, UI, or marketing
- Internal use within your organization

## Allowed Uses (With Conditions)

You may:

- fork and modify the codebase
- build commercial products using Tether
- white-label Tether as part of your own system
- offer hosted or SaaS versions based on Tether

Conditions:

- You must not present your product as the official Tether distribution.
- You must not imply endorsement or affiliation with Chrispian Burks or Hollis Labs.
- You must not use Tether branding in a way that causes confusion.

Examples:

- OK: "Acme Agent Runtime (built with Tether)"
- OK: "Custom control plane powered by Tether"
- Not OK: "Tether Enterprise by Acme"
- Not OK: "Official Tether Cloud"

## Prohibited Uses

The following are not allowed:

- Releasing a product named "Tether" that is not the official distribution
- Using the Tether name or branding as your product's primary identity
- Misrepresenting a fork as the official Tether project
- Using logos or branding that are confusingly similar to Tether

## Official Distribution

Only builds released by Chrispian Burks / Hollis Labs, or explicitly
designated maintainers, may be referred to as:

- Tether
- Official Tether
- Tether by Hollis Labs

## Naming Your Fork

If you create a fork or derivative, choose a different name.

You may reference Tether descriptively, for example:

- "<Your Product>, built on Tether"
- "<Your Product>, powered by Tether"

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use is acceptable, open an issue in this
repository.

## Summary (Non-Legal)

- Code: open under MIT
- Brand: protected
- Commercial use of the code: allowed
- Commercial use of the Tether brand: not implied
